At the [P4 Workshop 2025](https://p4.org/event/2025-p4-workshop/), the National Research Platform (NRP) presented its ongoing work on enabling wide-area programmable networking research through a federated infrastructure of advanced computing and networking resources. The presentation, **"Cross-Federated P4 Research Testbed for Wide-Area Programmable Networking Experiments,"** highlighted how NRP supports P4, SmartNIC, FPGA, and DPU-based experimentation at national and international scale.

**Presenter:** Mohammad Firas Sada, Data Science Research Specialist, San Diego Supercomputer Center

## Enabling Programmable Networking Research

NRP provides a distributed research testbed that connects more than a dozen institutions across multiple continents. It integrates diverse hardware—including **Tofino2 P4 switches**, **AMD Alveo SmartNICs**, and **NVIDIA BlueField-2 DPUs**—to support in-network computing and programmable data-plane research. The platform aims to lower barriers for researchers exploring network telemetry, in-network machine learning, and real-time data-plane processing.

The presentation showcased how NRP's federated infrastructure enables researchers to conduct experiments that would be impossible on single-site testbeds, providing access to:

- **Geographically distributed P4 switches** for wide-area network testing
- **High-performance compute clusters** (CPU, GPU, FPGA) integrated with programmable networking
- **Sub-nanosecond accuracy** telemetry and timestamping using FPGA-based packet capture
- **Cross-domain integration** with external facilities like ESnet SENSE and FABRIC

---

## Wide-Area P4 Testbed Capabilities

The NRP's infrastructure allows researchers to:

- **Deploy and test P4 programs** across geographically distributed sites
- **Combine programmable networking devices** with high-performance compute clusters (CPU, GPU, FPGA)
- **Conduct telemetry and timestamping experiments** with sub-nanosecond accuracy using FPGA-based packet capture
- **Integrate with external facilities** like ESnet SENSE and FABRIC for cross-domain networking experiments

This cross-federated model enables realistic evaluation of P4 and data-plane systems under wide-area network conditions—something rarely available to academic researchers.

## P4 Workshop 2025 Context

The [P4 Workshop 2025](https://p4.org/event/2025-p4-workshop/) was held on **October 13th** at the San Jose Convention Center in conjunction with the 2025 OCP Global Summit. The workshop featured both in-person and pre-recorded content, bringing together the P4 ecosystem to share knowledge, insights, and experiences across the broader community.

Key highlights from the workshop included:

- **Keynote presentations** on high-level transport programs and resilient GPU interconnect
- **Industry talks** from Google, Cisco, Intel, and AMD on P4 applications
- **Research presentations** covering topics from network security to AI-enabled infrastructure
- **Live demonstrations** including "Gigaflow: Pipeline-Aware Caching in Virtual Switches with P4"

## Collaboration and Next Steps

NRP continues to collaborate with the P4 community to expand access to programmable network hardware and develop reproducible workflows for data-plane experimentation. Upcoming work includes:

- **Extending SmartNIC/FPGA support** across the federated infrastructure
- **Publishing example configurations** for common P4 research scenarios
- **Coordinating with partner testbeds** for joint demonstrations
- **Developing educational materials** for programmable networking research

## About the P4 Language Consortium

The [P4 Language Consortium](https://p4.org/) is a community-driven organization focused on advancing programmable network data planes. The consortium brings together researchers, engineers, and practitioners to develop standards, tools, and best practices for programmable networking.
