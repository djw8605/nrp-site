The **National Research Platform (NRP)** had a standout presence at this year's **ACM/IEEE Supercomputing Conference (SC25)**, held in St. Louis, Missouri — starting with their Network Research Exhibition being honored with the **SC25 Spirit of Innovation Award**!

This recognition highlighted **Agentic AI with Qualcomm Cloud AI 100 Ultra Cards for HPC Cluster Management and Resource Provisioning**, underscoring NRP's commitment to applying intelligent, autonomous approaches to managing large-scale research infrastructure.

Throughout the week, NRP was featured across tutorials, technical sessions, live demos, and booth engagements — each pointing to how AI, networking, and modern orchestration technologies can come together to push the boundaries of scientific computing.

## 🏆 SC25 Spirit of Innovation Award

NRP's Network Research Exhibition was recognized with the prestigious **SC25 Spirit of Innovation Award** for its groundbreaking work on **Agentic AI with Qualcomm Cloud AI 100 Ultra Cards**. This award celebrates NRP's innovative approach to using intelligent, autonomous AI systems for HPC cluster management and resource provisioning, demonstrating how agentic AI can optimize large-scale research infrastructure operations.

The award-winning demonstration showcased how Qualcomm Cloud AI 100 Ultra Cards (GenAI Cards) can be integrated into NRP's distributed infrastructure to enable intelligent, self-managing cluster operations that adapt to workload demands in real time.

---

## 🌐 NRP in SCinet

Additionally, NRP was directly integrated into the conference's own infrastructure through **SCinet**, with SCinet's HPC cluster temporarily federated into the National Research Platform. This live integration enabled NRP services, Kubernetes orchestration, and AI-driven workflows to operate on conference-hosted resources in real time — a powerful example of on-demand, federated cyberinfrastructure in action.

NRP's participation in **SCinet**, the conference's dedicated high-speed research network, demonstrated the platform's capabilities in wide-area networking and distributed infrastructure. Through SCinet, NRP showcased:

- **Federated infrastructure** connecting multiple sites across the United States
- **Programmable networking** capabilities using P4, SmartNICs, and FPGA technologies
- **Real-time data processing** and in-network computing demonstrations
- **Cross-site collaboration** tools and workflows for distributed scientific computing
- **Live SCinet-connected Nautilus nodes** enabling seamless execution of high-performance, interactive workloads

## 📚 Network Research Exhibitions

NRP presented multiple Network Research Exhibitions (NREs) at SC25, demonstrating cutting-edge research in distributed cyberinfrastructure, programmable networking, and in-network computing:

### NRE 119: The National Research Platform and SCinet

**Enabling Live, Multi-Institutional Scientific AI/ML and HPC Workflows**

This NRE demonstrated the NRP as a living example of community-driven, federated cyberinfrastructure connecting AI, HPC, and data workflows across more than 70 institutions. The demonstration featured:

- Interactive AI workflows and scientific visualization live on the SCinet show floor via Nautilus nodes
- Reproducible, multi-user JupyterHub, Kubernetes, and SLURM-based access
- Heterogeneous hardware orchestration with Kubernetes, including GPUs, DPUs, and AMD/Xilinx Alveo and Intel Agilex and Stratix FPGAs
- Full production network connectivity with deterministic QoS across SCinet, StarLight, FABRIC, Caltech, and Chameleon Cloud using ESnet SENSE
- Scientific workloads including 3D Cell Reconstruction with AI, Digital Twins for Manufacturing and Space Exploration, Wildfire Monitoring, and LLM as a Service

> 📄 [View NRE 119 Abstract (PDF)](https://sc25.supercomputing.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/nre119s3.pdf)

### NRE 120: Live High-Precision Per-Packet Kubernetes Data Telemetry

**From NRP to StarLight via ESnet SENSE**

This demonstration linked NRP Kubernetes cluster resources at SDSC to the StarLight facility in Chicago via the ESnet SENSE Orchestrator. The system transmitted live Kubernetes network data packets to P4-programmable Tofino2 SmartNIC endpoints deployed at StarLight for advanced packet inspection, telemetry, and analysis.

Key features included:
- High-throughput (100Gbps) connectivity between NRP Kubernetes cluster at SDSC and StarLight in Chicago
- Real-time packet telemetry extraction and analysis using P4-programmable Tofino2 SmartNICs
- FPGA and DPDK acceleration streaming telemetry metadata into Kafka databases
- Dynamic detection of network congestion, packet loss, and anomalous traffic patterns

> 📄 [View NRE 120 Abstract (PDF)](https://sc25.supercomputing.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/nre120s3.pdf)

### NRE 121: Real-Time In-Network Machine Learning and P4 Testbed Deployment

**On FPGA SmartNICs, DPUs, and Switches**

This NRE highlighted a large-scale programmable testbed integrating P4-programmable SmartNICs, DPUs, and switches for real-time in-network computing research. The demonstration featured:

- Real-time machine learning inference embedded directly in the network data plane using fixed-point arithmetic and Taylor series approximations on AMD/Xilinx Alveo U55C FPGA SmartNICs
- Deployment of flexible, scalable P4 experimentation environment spanning geographically distributed sites
- Implementation of unprivileged P4-DPDK virtual switches using Kubernetes and Multus CNI
- Comprehensive P4 programming implementation on BlueField-2 DPUs using DOCA DPL with P4-16 support

> 📄 [View NRE 121 Abstract (PDF)](https://sc25.supercomputing.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/nre121s3.pdf)
