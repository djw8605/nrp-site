---
title: Training Join Links
description: Give workshop attendees time-limited LLM access with a single link
---

A training join link lets attendees get an NRP LLM API key without waiting for you to add them.
Anyone who opens the link and logs in to the NRP is added to your namespace and shown a key.
When the training ends, people the link added are removed again, and their keys stop working.

## Before you start

- You must be an admin of the namespace.
- The namespace must have LLM access enabled. Join links are not offered on other namespaces.
- If the namespace has Kubernetes enabled, attendees also get Kubernetes `edit` access to it until
  access ends. Use an LLM-only namespace if attendees should only get LLM access.

## Create a link

1. Open [Namespaces](/namespaces) and select the namespace.
2. In **Training join links**, enter a training name, the maximum number of attendees, when joins
   stop being accepted, and when access ends. Access can last at most 90 days.
3. Select **Create join link**, then copy the link from the table and share it with attendees.

## What attendees see

The link opens the [LLM API Keys](/llmtoken) page. Attendees log in to the NRP with their institution,
or another provider offered on the login page. Their first login creates their NRP account. They are
then added to the namespace and shown a new API key with the date their access ends.

If an attendee loses their key, they can open the link again. That replaces their training key and
doesn't use up another seat.

## When the training ends

- **Revoke** stops new joins. People who already joined keep access until it ends.
- **End now** stops new joins and removes attendees within 15 minutes.
- At the access end date, everyone the link added is removed from the namespace within 15 minutes,
  and their keys in the namespace are revoked a few minutes later. You get an email summary.

People who were already members of the namespace, people you added yourself, and namespace admins
are never removed. Attendees keep their NRP accounts, so their usage stays in NRP reports.
