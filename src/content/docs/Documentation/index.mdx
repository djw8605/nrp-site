The **National Research Platform (NRP)** is an NSF-funded computing
platform spanning more than 70 research sites. **Nautilus** is its main
Kubernetes cluster, and most of these pages describe it: you get access,
work inside a project namespace, then run GPU pods, batch jobs, or
notebooks on it.

## Your first job, step by step

<Steps>

1.  [Getting access](/documentation/userdocs/start/getting-started/) —
    sign in through CILogon and install `kubectl`.

2.  Join a project. Work runs inside a namespace your
    [Group Administrator](/documentation/userdocs/start/hierarchy/)
    creates; getting-started shows how to request one if you do not
    yet have it.

3.  Start with a [GPU pod](/documentation/userdocs/running/gpu-pods/)
    for interactive work, or a
    [batch job](/documentation/userdocs/running/jobs/) for runs that
    finish on their own.

4.  Keep your results on
    [persistent storage](/documentation/userdocs/storage/ceph/) and
    [move data in and out](/documentation/userdocs/storage/move-data/)
    when you are done.

</Steps>

:::caution[Data policy]
The National Research Platform currently has no storage that is
suitable for **HIPAA**, **PID**, **FISMA**, **FERPA**, or protected
data of any kind. Users are not permitted to store such data on NRP
machines. Unsure whether your data counts as protected?
[Ask us](/documentation/userdocs/start/support/) before you store it.
:::
